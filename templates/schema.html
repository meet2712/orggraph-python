A decision audit, not a second copy of the data

employee_reporting_audit joins an employee's raw fields to their inferred features, accepted manager, leading candidate, scores and decision reason. The answer key is kept in a separate CSV and is not part of this database.

SELECT full_name, inferred_manager, leading_candidate,
       confidence, margin, decision_reason
FROM employee_reporting_audit
WHERE company_id = 'cmp_0004'
  AND employee_id IN ('emp_0000542', 'emp_0000543')
ORDER BY employee_id;

Keys and relationships

One company can have many employees. The employee's company key refers to the company row; manager choices are calculated in views, so there is no stored manager foreign key.

What the database checks before serving

A newly inserted employee appears in the feature, prediction and audit views on the next query. Headcount, size band and coverage update with the same source change. Checks cover keys, accepted lines, decision counts, self-management, cross-company links, seniority and cycles.

The published site is a fixed export of the database at deployment time. Local employee edits remain local until a new snapshot is built and deployed.
